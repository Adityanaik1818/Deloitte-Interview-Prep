<h2 align="center">✦ 𝗧𝗛𝗘 𝗦𝗘𝗟𝗘𝗖𝗧𝗜𝗢𝗡 𝗣𝗥𝗢𝗖𝗘𝗦𝗦 ✦</h2>

The official selection pipeline is divided into four critical stages[cite: 8]:

| <div align="center">Stage</div> | <div align="center">Round</div> | <div align="center">Details</div> |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **01** | <kbd>Aptitude Test</kbd> | Conducted by Aspiring Minds (AMCAT)[cite: 8]. |
| **02** | <kbd>JAM Round</kbd> | "Just a Minute" impromptu speaking test[cite: 8]. |
| **03** | <kbd>Technical Interview</kbd> | Deep dive into your resume, projects, and core tech[cite: 8]. |
| **04** | <kbd>HR Interview</kbd> | Behavioral evaluation and company alignment[cite: 8]. |

<br>

---

<br>

<h2 align="center">✦ 𝗥𝗢𝗨𝗡𝗗 𝟭: 𝗔𝗠𝗖𝗔𝗧 𝗔𝗣𝗧𝗜𝗧𝗨𝗗𝗘 ✦</h2>

> **Format:** 120 minutes total duration[cite: 8]. Divided into Quants, Logical, and Verbal sections with 25 questions each[cite: 8]. All sections are generally considered easy and can be completed well before the time limit[cite: 8].

<table align="center" width="100%">
  <tr>

      <h3 align="center">📈 Quantitative</h3>
      <ul>
        <li>Simple & Compound Interest, Profit & Loss, Percentages[cite: 8].</li>
        <li>Permutation & Combinations, Probability (e.g., dice, tossing two coins)[cite: 8].</li>
        <li>Time/Work, Speed/Time/Distance, Pipes & Cisterns[cite: 8].</li>
        <li>Divisibility, Surds/Indices, LCM/HCF, Chain Rule[cite: 8].</li>
        <li>Area/Volumes, Number Series, Arrangements[cite: 8].</li>
        <li>⚠️ <b>CRITICAL:</b> Prepare heavily for <b>Logarithm</b> based questions, as they appear very frequently[cite: 8].</li>
      </ul>

<h2 align="center">✦ 𝗥𝗢𝗨𝗡𝗗 𝟮: 𝗝𝗔𝗠 (𝗝𝘂𝘀𝘁 𝗮 𝗠𝗶𝗻𝘂𝘁𝗲) ✦</h2>

<div align="center">
  <img src="https://img.icons8.com/fluency-systems-regular/64/86BC25/microphone.png" width="55"/><br>
</div>

* **The Selection:** Candidates pick a number from 1 to 80, which corresponds to a specific topic assigned by the interviewer[cite: 8].
* **The Timer:** You are given exactly **30 seconds** to prepare your thoughts and must speak continuously for **1 minute**[cite: 8].
* **Winning Strategy:** Maintain high confidence, avoid awkward pauses, and do not repeat sentences you have already used[cite: 8]. This round strictly tests your communication and presentation skills[cite: 8].

<br>

<h2 align="center">✦ 𝗥𝗢𝗨𝗡𝗗𝗦 𝟯 & 𝟰: 𝗧𝗘𝗖𝗛𝗡𝗜𝗖𝗔𝗟 & 𝗛𝗥 ✦</h2>

This stage involves an in-depth discussion of the projects listed on your resume and your technical domain knowledge, followed immediately by behavioral HR questions[cite: 8].

### 💻 Common Technical Questions (Java Focus)
* What exactly do you mean by multithreading in JAVA?[cite: 8]
* How can a deadlock occur in a system, and how would you break it?[cite: 8]
* Explain the significance of the `volatile` keyword[cite: 8].
* Name a feature in J2EE that you like and explain why[cite: 8].
* How do you create threads in JAVA? Detail the thread methods[cite: 8].

### 🤝 Common HR Questions
* Tell me 3 objectives you would focus on if I gave you this job[cite: 8].
* How do you like our interview process so far, and why?[cite: 8]
* Out of all companies, why Deloitte?[cite: 8]
* What are your long-term future plans within this firm?[cite: 8]

<h2 align="center">✦ 𝗜𝗡𝗦𝗜𝗗𝗘𝗥 𝗜𝗡𝗧𝗘𝗥𝗩𝗜𝗘𝗪 𝗧𝗜𝗣𝗦 ✦</h2>

> **1.** Be confident and relaxed; remember that you are simply looking for a better opportunity[cite: 8].<br>
> **2.** **NEVER bluff.** Do not include false information on your CV; expect questions based *specifically* on the skills you listed[cite: 8].<br>
> **3.** Try to steer the conversation to bring the interviewer into your comfort zone[cite: 8].<br>
> **4.** Practice and brush up on your **SQL** concepts thoroughly before sitting for the interview[cite: 8].
