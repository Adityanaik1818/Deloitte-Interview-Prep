<h2 align="center" id="-the-selection-pipeline">✦ 𝗧𝗛𝗘 𝗦𝗘𝗟𝗘𝗖𝗧𝗜𝗢𝗡 𝗣𝗜𝗣𝗘𝗟𝗜𝗡𝗘 ✦</h2>

<p align="center">The official selection process follows a strict four-stage pipeline[cite: 8]:</p>

<div align="center">

`01. AMCAT Aptitude` ➔ `02. JAM Round` ➔ `03. Technical Interview` ➔ `04. HR Interview`

</div>

<br>

---

<br>

<h2 align="center" id="-round-1-amcat-aptitude">✦ 𝗥𝗢𝗨𝗡𝗗 𝟭: 𝗔𝗠𝗖𝗔𝗧 𝗔𝗣𝗧𝗜𝗧𝗨𝗗𝗘 ✦</h2>

> **Test Dynamics:** The test is 120 minutes long and is divided into Quants, Logical, and Verbal sections, each containing 25 questions[cite: 8]. All sections are generally considered easy and can be completed before the time limit[cite: 8].

<table align="center" width="100%">
  <tr>
    <td width="33%" valign="top">
      <div align="center">
        <img src="https://img.icons8.com/ios-filled/64/86BC25/math.png" width="45"/><br>
        <h3>📊 Quants</h3>
      </div>
      <p>✔️ <b>Core:</b> Simple and Compound Interest, Profit & Loss, Percentages, Time and Work[cite: 8].</p>
      <p>✔️ <b>Advanced:</b> Permutation and Combinations, Probability (single dice, tossing two coins)[cite: 8].</p>
      <p>✔️ <b>Misc:</b> Divisibility, Chain rule, Surds & indices, LCM & HCF, Speed/time, Area and volumes, Number series, Pipes and cisterns, Arrangements[cite: 8].</p>
      <p>🚨 <b>CRITICAL:</b> Prepare well for Logarithm-based questions, as they appear frequently[cite: 8].</p>
    </td>
    <td width="33%" valign="top">
      <div align="center">
        <img src="https://img.icons8.com/ios-filled/64/86BC25/brain.png" width="45"/><br>
        <h3>🧩 Logical</h3>
      </div>
      <p>✔️ <b>Series & Deductions:</b> Odd man out series, Conclusion deduction, Theme deductions, Syllogisms[cite: 8].</p>
      <p>✔️ <b>Arrangements:</b> General arrangement questions (e.g., matching 5 people to birth months and favorite foods)[cite: 8].</p>
      <p>✔️ <b>Visual/Analytical:</b> Blood relations, Direction sense, Clocks, Calendars, Puzzles, Data interpretation[cite: 8].</p>
    </td>
    <td width="33%" valign="top">
      <div align="center">
        <img src="https://img.icons8.com/ios-filled/64/86BC25/language.png" width="45"/><br>
        <h3>📝 Verbal</h3>
      </div>
      <p>✔️ <b>Comprehension:</b> 2 passages (e.g., on birth scenarios in India and the Indian education system)[cite: 8].</p>
      <p>✔️ <b>Grammar:</b> Basic grammar, Fill in the blanks, Sentence ordering, Sentence correction, Spelling errors, Tenses[cite: 8].</p>
      <p>✔️ <b>Vocabulary:</b> Synonyms, Antonyms, Parajumbles, One-word substitutes[cite: 8].</p>
    </td>
  </tr>
</table>

<h2 align="center" id="-round-2-jam-just-a-minute">✦ 𝗥𝗢𝗨𝗡𝗗 𝟮: 𝗝𝗔𝗠 (𝗝𝘂𝘀𝘁 𝗔 𝗠𝗶𝗻𝘂𝘁𝗲) ✦</h2>

<table align="center" width="100%">
  <tr>
    <td width="20%" align="center">
      <img src="https://img.icons8.com/ios-filled/100/86BC25/microphone.png" />
    </td>
    <td width="80%">
      <ul>
        <li><b>The Setup:</b> Candidates pick a number from 1 to 80, which corresponds to a specific topic assigned by the interviewer[cite: 8].</li>
        <li><b>The Rules:</b> You are given 30 seconds to prepare and must talk for JUST A MINUTE[cite: 8].</li>
        <li><b>The Strategy:</b> Do not pause, and do not repeat the same sentences you've used[cite: 8]. They check your communication and presentation skills; confidence is the key here[cite: 8]!</li>
      </ul>
    </td>
  </tr>

<h2 align="center" id="-insider-pro-tips">✦ 𝗜𝗡𝗦𝗜𝗗𝗘𝗥 𝗣𝗥𝗢 𝗧𝗜𝗣𝗦 ✦</h2>

<div align="center">

`🛡️ Be Confident` • `🚫 No CV Bluffs` • `🧠 Control the Comfort Zone` • `🗄️ Master SQL`

</div>

> * **Confidence:** Be confident and relaxed; remember, you are looking for a better opportunity[cite: 8].
> * **Honesty:** Don't give any bluff in CV[cite: 8]. They will ask you questions from your CV specific skills, so don't write anything you don't know[cite: 8].
> * **Control:** Try to bring the interviewer into your comfort zone rather than his[cite: 8].
> * **Tech Prep:** Practice SQL a bit before the interview[cite: 8].
